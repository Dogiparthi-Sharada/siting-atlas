THINGS THAT WORK WHEN A NORMAL SEARCH FAILS - please actually try these:

- Search the ADDRESS ALONE in quotes, with no mention of Amazon.
  Often a leasing listing, a permit, or a trucking directory names the site.
- Search the address plus "DSP" or "delivery service partner". Delivery
  stations are surrounded by small contractor firms that publish the address
  of the station they load at. This found several already.
- Search the address plus "square feet" or "sq ft". Size settles it:
  under 250,000 is a delivery station, over 600,000 is a fulfillment centre.
- Search the address on hiring.amazon.com. The JOB TITLE gives it away:
  "Delivery Station Warehouse Associate" vs "Fulfillment Center Warehouse
  Associate" vs "Sortation Center Associate".
- Search the city's planning or zoning agenda for the street name.
- Try warehouse.ninja, zonhack, selleressentials, mapcarta, warehouse worker
  network - these directories list Amazon codes against addresses.

- Quote a sentence naming the street address or the city. Give the URL.

- If the site is an OFFICE, DATA CENTRE, WHOLE FOODS or a corporate building,
  say so - that is a useful answer, not a failure.
- If you find the opening year too, add it. Bonus, not required.
