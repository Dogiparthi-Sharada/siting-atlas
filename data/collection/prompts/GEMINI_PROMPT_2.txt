Find the opening year for each Amazon delivery station location below.
These are numbered 7 to 35, continuing from a previous list.

FORMAT - one line each, nothing else:

  7. 2020 Q3 - opened - "exact quote from the source naming the street or
     neighbourhood and the date" - https://url
  8. 2019 - opened - "quote" - https://url
  9. UNKNOWN

RULES:

- Quote the source. One sentence that names the STREET ADDRESS or the
  NEIGHBOURHOOD and the DATE. If you cannot quote it, it does not count.
- If you cannot find a real dated source, write UNKNOWN. Do not estimate.
  A blank is useful. A guessed date is worse than useless.
- Do NOT use manifest.supplystudies.com. It cannot be verified.
- Do not give me Amazon facility codes. Match on ZIP and coordinates only.
- Quarter only if you know the month. Year alone is fine.
- Write "announced" instead of "opened" if the source only says Amazon
  planned, leased, or proposed the site.
- If a site CLOSED, say so and give the year. Closures matter as much as
  openings.
- If a site is actually a fulfillment centre or sortation centre rather than
  a delivery station, say so.

WHAT GOOD LOOKS LIKE - two that were accepted:

  "Amazon delivery stations recently opened in Downers Grove and West
   Chicago" - CBS Chicago, 28 September 2020
  "in October, opened a 245,000-square-foot Prime delivery distribution
   center at 3601 Howard Street" - Village of Skokie newsletter, Winter 2020

Both name a place and a date. That is the bar.

official municipal newsletters; local TV and newspapers; The Guardian;
commercial real-estate press. Local outlets for these areas: Denver Post,
BizWest, Colorado Real Estate Journal, QNS and Crain's New York, Mercury
News and SF Chronicle and Silicon Valley Business Journal, Seattle Times and
Puget Sound Business Journal and The News Tribune, Phoenix Business Journal
and AZCentral.
