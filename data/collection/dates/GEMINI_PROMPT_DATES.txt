The kinds of date, best first. Give me the best one you can find, not all of

   CERTIFICATE OF OCCUPANCY  the building may legally be occupied. Closest

Where to look, in order - please actually try these:

   - the COUNTY ASSESSOR for that county, searching the street address.
     Every US county publishes "year built" per parcel because it drives the
     tax bill. This is usually the fastest hit.
   - the CITY OR COUNTY BUILDING PERMIT PORTAL. Most run Accela Citizen
     Access, eTRAKiT, CitizenServe or Tyler EnerGov. Search the street
     number and street name only - "17341 Minnezona" finds more than
     "17341 West Minnezona Avenue".
   - LOCAL NEWS. Amazon delivery station openings are almost always covered
     by the local paper or the local business journal. Search the address
     plus "Amazon" plus "delivery station".
   - the permit portal again, searching the APPLICANT or OWNER field rather
     than the address, for: Amazon, Amazon.com Services, Amazon Logistics,
     Amazon.com Services LLC. Amazon usually LEASES, so also try the
     landlord: Prologis, Duke Realty, First Industrial, STAG Industrial.

Three rules, and the first one matters most:

   - I have given you a date in the last column called MUST BE BEFORE. That
     is a hard upper bound: a government inspection proved the building was
     already operating on that day. Any date you give me MUST be earlier
     than it. If the only date you can find is LATER, tell me so explicitly
     and give it anyway, marked IMPOSSIBLE - that tells me something is
     wrong with either the record or my bound, and I want to know.

   - NOT FOUND is a good answer. Do not guess, do not estimate, and do not
     give me a date for a different building on the same street. A wrong
     date is worse than no date, because nothing downstream can tell it is
     wrong.

   - MANY OF THESE BUILDINGS EXISTED BEFORE AMAZON MOVED IN. If the
     assessor's "year built" is much earlier than my upper bound, say so and
     give me BOTH: the year the building was built, and the date Amazon took
     it over if you can find it. The second is the one I actually want. It
     is usually a tenant improvement permit or a news story.
